Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and login credentials when you create an account, as well as the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity within the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the core features of Spendly: logging expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and maintaining your account. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand how the app is used and to improve its features. We do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical and administrative safeguards. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party services for functions such as hosting or email delivery, and those providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided within the app.
